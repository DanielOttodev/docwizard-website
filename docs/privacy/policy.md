# DocWizard Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 August 2026**

DocWizard is provided by DOTTO WEB SOLUTIONS (ABN/ACN 25 588 779 536 ), an Australian company based in Victoria, Australia ("DocWizard", "we", "us" or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect personal information when you use the DocWizard website, application, APIs and related services (the "Service").

## 1. Information We Collect

The personal information we collect depends on how you interact with DocWizard.

### Account information

When you create or use an account, we may collect information such as:

* your name;
* email address;
* organisation or company name;
* account identifiers;
* authentication information; and
* account settings.

Authentication is provided using Clerk. Authentication credentials and related information may therefore also be processed by Clerk in providing authentication services.

### Billing information

If you subscribe to a paid DocWizard plan, payment processing is provided by Stripe.

We may receive information relating to your subscription and payment status, such as:

* billing name;
* billing address;
* subscription details;
* transaction identifiers;
* payment status; and
* limited payment-method information.

DocWizard does not ordinarily receive or store complete payment card numbers.

### Customer Content

When using DocWizard, customers may upload or provide:

* document templates;
* generated documents;
* data used to populate documents;
* files and other content; and
* information submitted through the DocWizard API.

Customer Content may contain personal information about the customer, its employees, clients, customers or other individuals.

The customer determines what Customer Content it submits to DocWizard.

### Technical and usage information

When you access DocWizard, we may automatically collect information such as:

* IP address;
* browser and device information;
* operating system;
* pages and features accessed;
* dates and times of access;
* API requests;
* error and diagnostic information; and
* security and operational logs.

### Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website and Service.

Google Analytics may collect information about your device, browser, interactions with the website and other usage information, including through cookies and similar technologies.

Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential analytics cookies.

## 2. How We Collect Information

We may collect personal information:

* directly from you when you register or communicate with us;

We may use personal information to:

* create and administer accounts;
* authenticate users;
* provide document generation and storage functionality;
* process API requests;
* provide customer support;
* process subscriptions and payments;
* monitor usage and enforce plan limits;
* maintain and improve the Service;

Customers are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary rights, notices, consents or other lawful basis to provide personal information to DocWizard and use it through the Service.

DocWizard does not currently send Customer Content, uploaded templates or generated documents to third-party generative AI providers for AI model training or generation.

If this practice changes materially in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide appropriate notice where required.

## 6. Storage and Security

DocWizard uses Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure to operate the Service, including services such as Amazon S3, Amazon RDS and Amazon ECS.

Uploaded templates and generated documents may be stored using Amazon S3.

We implement technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or misuse.

These safeguards include measures such as encrypted network communications, access controls and encryption of stored document files.

No system can guarantee absolute security, and customers should also take appropriate steps to protect their accounts and API credentials.
